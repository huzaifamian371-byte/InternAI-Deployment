💡 High-Score Resume Strategies

  • Use the Google X-Y-Z formula: "Accomplished [X] as measured by [Y], by doing [Z]".
  • Lead with strong action verbs: "Architected", "Engineered", "Optimized", rather than "Assisted" or "Worked on".
  • Quantify outcomes: Mention latency reductions, unit test coverage percentages, or user numbers.
  • Clean skill categorization: Group technical competencies by languages, databases, web, and tools.
